Channel mapping

Hue and saturation operate in HSL space. Contrast, brightness, warmth, inversion, grayscale, and channel swaps operate directly on RGB pixels.

What to notice

Channel swaps are structural: red, green, and blue information trade places. Hue shifts rotate color relationships while preserving more of the original luminance.

Why it exists

I like art and color theory, and I kept needing quick color transformations without signing up for another account or fighting a bad free tool. This is the utility I wanted to have open.
