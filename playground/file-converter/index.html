Power-user note: image output stays browser-native. MP3, FLAC, and video presets lazy-load the single-thread FFmpeg.wasm core from CDN, then run locally in your browser. Large video files can be slow and memory-heavy.

Why it exists

Useful online file converters often require an account or are hosted on websites with questionable privacy and security practices, along with slow upload times or tiny usage limits. This page is made for fast local conversions with privacy and speed in mind. If you don’t want to take my word for it, you can always check the repository yourself :3 (or have an LLM inspect it for you…).

What it can do

Images convert through canvas to PNG, JPEG, or WebP when the browser can decode the input. Audio can export as WAV, MP3, or FLAC, and video supports MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI, and frame export.
